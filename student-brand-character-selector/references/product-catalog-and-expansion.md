# Product Catalog and Expansion

Use this reference when the user asks what can be made with the selected student brand and character, or when the workflow must move from naming/character selection to sellable sticker, printable, stationery, school-event, or promotional-goods products.

## Core rule

Do not recommend every possible product equally. Prioritize products that can be produced with low capital, low inventory risk, fast design iteration, clear student/parent/teacher use scenes, and strong bundle expansion. Public sale remains blocked until market/IP/visual/print/operator proof is complete.

## Brand-character fit rules

| Brand lane | Best products | Avoid at first |
|---|---|---|
| 칭찬/보상 | reward board, praise labels, mission coupons | expensive hard goods |
| 학교/교실 | class coupons, name labels, seat tags, teacher labels | overly babyish characters |
| 습관/공부 | checklist stickers, study planner, reading board | guaranteed education claims |
| 캐릭터/IP | sticker packs, postcards, package labels, seasonal goods | high-MOQ goods before proof |
| 선물/행사 | thank-you cards, party labels, admission/graduation stickers | complex custom manufacturing |

## Product scoring add-on

When recommending products, score each product using 100 points:

| Metric | Points |
|---|---:|
| buyer pain clarity | 15 |
| first-production simplicity | 15 |
| design reuse from selected character | 12 |
| low inventory/MOQ risk | 12 |
| bundle and series expansion | 12 |
| seasonal/event demand | 10 |
| repeat purchase potential | 10 |
| smartstore thumbnail clarity | 8 |
| IP/claims safety | 6 |

Interpretation:
- 82+: first launch candidate.
- 72-81: second-wave candidate.
- 60-71: prototype only.
- under 60: do not prioritize yet.

## Launch sequencing

1. Start with SKU-01 reward sticker board set.
2. Add SKU-02 checklist pack and SKU-03 name labels only after the first design style is approved.
3. Add teacher/class products when teacher-facing wording is approved.
4. Add event/seasonal products around 입학, 어린이날, 여름방학, 2학기 시작, 크리스마스.
5. Add hard goods such as keyrings only after character demand and review evidence exists.
